AI for Small Business Permit Navigation
Client: City of San José, Municipal Permitting and Small Business Services

Team: Jose Garcia, Elias Duong, Ishaan Pabla, Jovin Duong, Aug–Oct 2026

My part: I lead the replit devlopment workflows and core problem statements analyzing municipal permitting workflows.

The problem
Small business owners in San José face fragmented, opaque, and multi-departmental requirements when trying to secure municipal operating permits. Today, navigating these complex administrative hurdles leads to severe project launch delays, widespread confusion, and high barriers for new entrepreneurs.

What we built
The prototype aggregates and structures fragmented municipal permit guidelines into a unified, searchable discovery framework using a chat bot to pull user data and push it to city workers to verify.

The user sees a clear, step-by-step compliance roadmap tailored to their specific business category and location within the city.

A human city official still validates, reviews, and grants final approval for all official permit applications and regulatory exceptions.

What I would do next
I found a gap in tracking real-time status updates across legacy municipal backend databases; next, I would build an integrated notification pipeline to seamlessly sync application milestones with applicants.
