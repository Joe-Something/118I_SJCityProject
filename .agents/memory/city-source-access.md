---
name: San José source access
description: Limits of public City pages as live permit-reference sources
---

Public City permit guidance pages returned HTTP 403 to server requests in this environment, while the City open-data CKAN catalog remained accessible. Do not equate an official-looking link or a successful daily dataset download with current, project-specific rules, fees, notices or individual status.

**Why:** Public web pages do not provide a documented machine-readable requirements interface here, and the daily open-data snapshot has a different purpose from the applicant-facing permit lookup.

**How to apply:** Fail closed when page checks fail; keep a timestamped provenance trail and direct users to the official page. Obtain explicit City-approved feeds or access before claiming comprehensive, real-time verification.

For this prototype, the user chose fictional stand-ins instead of blocked City documentation. Keep synthetic example version/date and the official verification destination distinct; never treat a synthetic document as City provenance.

**Why:** This enables demo interactions without fabricating official approval or current municipal requirements.

**How to apply:** Make the synthetic label prominent in answers and reference lists; keep real City links for independent verification only.
