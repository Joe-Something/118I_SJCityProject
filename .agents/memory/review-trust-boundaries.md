---
name: Review trust boundaries
description: Product wording and testing controls must not imply unimplemented City authority
---

Removing prototype or demo branding does not remove the need to identify synthetic references and distinguish internal review actions from official City decisions. Development role simulations must remain UI context controls, not a source of server authorization.

**Why:** The workspace has synthetic reference material and independently saved submissions, not an authenticated City permit-issuance, hearing-scheduling, dispatch, or email-delivery connection. Relabeling those capabilities as operational would misrepresent what actually occurred.

**How to apply:** Keep sample-data and non-official-decision disclosures even when improving enterprise-facing language. Use the existing Clerk private-metadata authorization for private staff operations; do not grant access through a browser toggle. Describe in-app messages separately from external delivery.
